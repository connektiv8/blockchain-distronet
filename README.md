# blockchain-distronet

This is the backend package of the Ginky AI Crypto distronet.

It is essentially designed to distribute (and re-distribute) earnings from subscription sales of Ginky AI
cross-network into various different crypto networks based on exchange rates.

The first aim of the blockchain-distronet is to taken revenue as part of the purchase of subscriptions,
and allocate a percentage of revenue into a "pooled fund", which is freely available to unregistered users
utilizing GinkyAI (essentially our form of "gas", to allow people to use GinkyAI for free).

Basically our distronet means that rather than our organisation keeping 100% of the revenue, 25% of it is
reinvested in allowing people to use GinkyAI for free (with limitations, as they are not logged-in users).

The second aim of our blockchain-distronet is to automatically maintain a parity between our links to "real"
/ fiat currencies in the form of exchange rates and ETH/USD pricing.

Our crypto token, GinkyAI (GIN), is pegged to ETH and therefore

distribute convert it into different cryptocurrencies and maintain a balanced and diverse portfolio.
